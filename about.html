I design brand systems, and I make things with my hands.

I'm a brand designer in San Francisco with eight years of experience across B2B software, AI, healthcare, and consumer brands. I've built identities from scratch, repositioned brand strategies, and run the brand system for a global scientific software company. I also spent two years in-house at Breg, a regulated health company, where I art directed an outside agency and presented creative decisions to senior leadership.

Away from the screen, I race downhill skateboards, work on skate shoots and edits, throw pots and build furniture. Making physical things keeps me excited about the creative process and teaches me about materials, textures, and more that I try to carry into my design work.

MidDesign is the name I work under as an independent designer. The name comes from an idea I keep coming back to: living in the middle of design. My design process is about embracing the process and remaining open to the natural flow of ideas so that I can create more authentic connections with audiences.
